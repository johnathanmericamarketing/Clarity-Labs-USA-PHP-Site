## Change ID: STORE-SUPPORT-20260814-002

**System:** Storefront
**Component:** Support
**Date:** 2026-08-14
**Title:** "My Support Tickets" page in the customer account portal

**Problem**
After submitting a ticket, customers had no view of their tickets inside their account — the only way back to a ticket was the confirmation email's "View Ticket & Reply" link. Johnathan requested tickets be visible under the account's Support tab so customers can check status and add more information.

**Cause**
Not previously built. The ticket system stored everything (public_token, comments) but no customer-facing list existed; the account sidebar "Support" link pointed at the public new-ticket form.

**Change Made**
Full pipeline (both sites):
- **Ops (API):** new authenticated endpoint `GET /api/v1/support/tickets` (`SupportApiController::myTickets`, inside the `customer.token` middleware group). Returns the customer's tickets matched by `customer_id` OR `reporter_email` (covers email-sourced tickets), with ticket_number, subject, status, type, priority, non-internal reply count, `public_url`, timestamps. Capped at 100, newest first. (Ops commit c599811, note OPS-SUPPORTAPI-20260814-001.)
- **Storefront:** new `shop/account/support.php` — behind `access_guard()` (login required), fetches tickets with the customer's bearer token via new `ClarityApiClient::getMyTickets()`, renders a table (ticket #, subject, opened date, status badge, replies count) with a **View & Reply →** link per ticket opening the ticket's public tracking page (same page as the email button), plus an **Open New Ticket** button to /support/.
- **Storefront:** account sidebar "Support" links in `shop/account/index.php`, `addresses.php`, `wishlist.php` now point to `/account/support` instead of `/support/`. The order-detail "Contact Support" link still goes to `/support/?subject=Order+…` (it opens a NEW prefilled ticket — correct behavior). The sign-in page and shop "Request a Product" links still go to the public form.

**Why This Change Was Made**
Customers should be able to find and follow up on their tickets from their account without digging through email. Reusing the proven public ticket page for the conversation avoided rebuilding a reply UI and keeps one thread surface for customer + staff.

**Files / Areas Affected**
- Storefront (commit 2faa252): `shop/account/support.php` (new), `includes/api-client.php` (getMyTickets), `shop/account/index.php`, `shop/account/addresses.php`, `shop/account/wishlist.php` (sidebar links)
- Ops (commit c599811 on branch claude/clarity-labs-shop-support-36ebd1): `app/Http/Controllers/Api/V1/SupportApiController.php`, `routes/api.php`

**Before**
Account sidebar "Support" → public new-ticket form only; no ticket list anywhere in the account.

**After**
Account sidebar "Support" → "My Support Tickets" list with status badges and View & Reply links; empty state and API-failure state handled; "Open New Ticket" always available.

**Testing / Validation**
- `php -l` clean on all changed files (both repos).
- Not yet deployed — production verification pending (see Follow-Up).

**Impact**
Customers can self-serve ticket status and continue conversations from their account. No change for logged-out users (public form unchanged).

**Open Questions / Follow-Up**
- Deploy ops (merge claude/clarity-labs-shop-support-36ebd1 → main, standard ops deploy incl. `php artisan route:clear`) BEFORE or together with storefront deploy — the account page degrades gracefully ("couldn't load your tickets") if the endpoint isn't live yet.
- Deploy storefront to the shop VPS path (backup first, reload php*-fpm).
- Production test: account → Support tab shows TKT-000024 for claritylabsusa@gmail.com.
